Aperto

A free manual camera for iPhone. Questions, bugs or ideas: help@vairyapp.com

How do I shoot RAW?

Tap the format button in the top-left corner and choose RAW, RAW+HEIC or RAW+JPEG. With Natural processing Aperto keeps the sensor's own DNG; with Apple processing, Pro iPhones capture Apple ProRAW. Some lenses (for example, some front cameras) don't offer RAW — Aperto then saves a HEIC and tells you.

How does Night mode work?

In the dark it turns on by itself: the moon button lights up. Hold still while the ring around the shutter fills — Aperto merges up to 16 RAW frames into one clean photo, without AI. On a stand it uses longer frames. Tap the moon to switch between Auto (A), On and Off.

How do I edit a photo?

Tap the thumbnail, then the slider button. Light, colour, detail, looks and crop; RAW files are re-developed. Save adds an edited copy to Photos — the original is never changed. Before your first shot, the thumbnail opens a photo from your library.

How do I open Aperto with Camera Control?

On iPhone 16 and later: Settings › Camera › Camera Control › Launch Camera, then choose Aperto. It opens with the button, even from the Lock Screen.

Where are my photos?

In the Photos app. A RAW+HEIC shot appears as one photo with a RAW badge.

Why can't I change EV?

Exposure compensation works while ISO and shutter are automatic. When you set them manually, the EV chip turns into a meter that shows how far you are from a normal exposure. Tap AUTO to return to automatic exposure.

Do looks change my RAW files?

No. Looks and 1:1 / 16:9 framing are applied to the HEIC or JPEG only. RAW files are always saved untouched and full-frame.

Is Aperto really free?

Yes, every feature. Tips in Settings are optional and unlock nothing.
